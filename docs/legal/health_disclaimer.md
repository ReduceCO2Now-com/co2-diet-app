# Health Disclaimer

**Last updated: August 4, 2026**

## Not a Medical Device

**This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any condition.**

CO2 Diet is a personal tracking tool. It helps you keep a record of the food you eat and gives
you estimates of your calorie intake, macronutrient intake, and the carbon footprint of your
food choices. Nothing in this App is, or should be understood as, professional medical, dietary,
or nutritional advice.

## Estimates, Not Prescriptions

The daily calorie and macronutrient targets shown in this App are calculated using standard,
published formulas (such as the Mifflin-St Jeor equation) based on the information you provide
in your profile. These figures are **estimates** intended as a general reference point — they
are not a personalized clinical assessment, and they will not be exactly correct for every body
or every situation. Similarly, the CO2e figures shown for foods are modeled estimates drawn from
food-category data and third-party databases, displayed with a confidence indicator (High /
Medium / Low) to reflect that uncertainty honestly. The App never presents a single false-
precision number as if it were a certainty.

The App helps you track your intake and see patterns over time — it does not tell you what you
should weigh, and it is not designed to help you "lose weight" as a goal in itself. Any goals
you set within the App (calorie targets, weight targets) are entirely your own choice, and you
should approach them with the same judgment you would apply to any self-directed health-related
activity.

## Not a Substitute for Professional Guidance

This App is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare provider, registered
dietitian, or other professional. If you have — or think you might have — a medical condition,
a nutritional deficiency, an eating disorder, or any other health concern, please speak with a
qualified professional before making decisions based on information in this App. If you are
pregnant, nursing, managing a chronic condition, or under the care of a healthcare provider for
any reason, consult that provider before setting or changing any calorie or weight goal in this
App.

## Safety Notices Built Into the App

To support safe use, the App includes built-in checks that show an informational warning (not a
hard block) if you enter a daily calorie target below a generally-recognized safe minimum, or a
weight goal that would put your estimated body mass index in an unusually low range. These
warnings are designed to make sure you've seen relevant information and a support resource
before continuing — they respect that you may be working with a healthcare provider on a
specific, supervised plan, so they never prevent you from proceeding once you've acknowledged
the warning.

## If You're Concerned About Eating or Your Relationship with Food

If you are concerned about your own or someone else's relationship with food or eating, please
reach out to a support service. You do not need to be in crisis to contact these resources —
they are there for anyone with questions or concerns.

**Germany:**
- **BZgA (Bundeszentrale für gesundheitliche Aufklärung) eating disorder counseling line** —
  0221 892031 — [bzga-essstoerungen.de](https://www.bzga-essstoerungen.de)
- **ANAD e.V.** (München) — 089 2199730 — [anad.de](https://www.anad.de)

**International:**
- [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) — a directory of vetted, free crisis and
  support lines covering 175+ countries, for readers outside Germany.
