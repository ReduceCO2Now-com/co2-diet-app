# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 4, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains what data CO2 Diet ("the App") collects, how it is used, and your
rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable data-protection
law. This document is the in-app copy of our Privacy Policy. A publicly hosted version at a
stable URL (required for App Store / Play Store listings) is a separate pre-submission item
tracked outside this document — we have not fabricated a hosting URL here.

## 1. Our Core Privacy Principle

CO2 Diet is designed to be **privacy-first by default**. In its current version, the App
operates entirely in **Local Mode**: all of your personal data is stored only on your own
device, in a local database. We do not operate any server that receives, stores, or processes
your personal data. There are **zero third-party analytics SDKs, zero advertising SDKs, and
zero behavioral-tracking SDKs** anywhere in the App.

## 2. Network Requests We Make

**This app makes network requests to (1) the Open Food Facts API for food search and barcode
lookups. It makes NO requests to any other service in Local Mode.**

When you search for a food item or scan a barcode, the App may send your search term or the
scanned barcode to the Open Food Facts API (a nonprofit, crowd-sourced food-product database)
to retrieve product information. Open Food Facts's own privacy policy governs how they process
that request. We do not send any other personal data (your name, your logged meals, your weight
history, your device identifiers) to Open Food Facts or to any other third party.

If you have no network connection, the App continues to work fully offline using its bundled
local food database — network access is only used to supplement search results, never required
for core functionality.

## 3. What Data Is Stored, and Where

The following data types are stored **only in the local database on your device**, and are
never transmitted anywhere by us:

- Your profile (age, sex, height, weight, activity level, and calculated targets)
- Logged meals, custom foods, and favorites
- Weight-tracking entries and goals
- App settings and preferences (including CO2 calculation profile factors)
- Your consent history (when you accepted these Terms/Privacy Policy/Health Disclaimer, and
  which policy version)

Because this data lives only on your device, we — the App's developers — have no ability to
see, access, or process this data on our end. If you uninstall the App without first exporting
your data, that data is permanently lost; we cannot recover it for you.

## 4. Your Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR (and equivalent regional law), you have the following rights. Because your data is
stored only on your device, most of these rights are exercised directly within the App, not by
contacting us:

- **Right of access & data portability (Art. 15, Art. 20 GDPR):** Use the App's **Export Data**
  feature (Settings) to export your data as a file at any time.
- **Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR):** Edit or correct any logged entry, custom food, or
  profile field directly within the App at any time.
- **Right to erasure / consent withdrawal (Art. 17 GDPR, Art. 7(3) GDPR):** Use the App's
  **Danger Zone → Delete all local data** feature (Settings) to permanently erase all your data
  from the device, or simply uninstall the App.
- **Right to object / restrict processing:** Because there is no processing performed by us
  outside your device, this right is inherently satisfied — you always retain full local
  control.

These mechanisms already exist in the App; we have not built any new "exercise your rights"
screen because the underlying capability is already present.

## 5. Data Retention

Your data is retained locally on your device for as long as you keep the App installed, or
until you delete it yourself via the Danger Zone feature. We hold no copy of your data and
therefore have no separate retention schedule to disclose.

## 6. Age Requirement

The App includes an optional self-declaration checkbox on the Legal Consent screen confirming
you are 16 years of age or older, consistent with the general age of digital consent under
German implementation of GDPR Art. 8. If you are under 16, please use the App only with the
involvement of a parent or legal guardian.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the App's functionality or applicable
law. The frontmatter `version` field at the top of this document's source file reflects the date
this Policy was last revised, and this version string is recorded alongside every consent event
in your local consent history.

## 8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please see the Impressum section of the Legal
& Privacy hub within the App for our current contact details.
