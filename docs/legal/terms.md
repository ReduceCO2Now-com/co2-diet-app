## 2. Description of the Service

CO2 Diet is a free, offline-first mobile application that helps you track the food you eat,
your nutritional intake, and the estimated carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e) footprint of your
food choices. In its current version, the App operates entirely in **Local Mode**: all of your

device. The App does not require you to create an account, and it does not upload your personal

The App does make network requests to the Open Food Facts API to search for food products and
look up barcodes. See our Privacy Policy for full details of this network activity.
