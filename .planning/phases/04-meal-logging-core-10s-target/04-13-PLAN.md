  <how-to-verify>
    **Devices:** one mid-range Android device (e.g. Galaxy Tab S7 FE or equivalent) and one physical iPhone via TestFlight — both required. Airplane mode ON for all core-flow testing (LOG-12).

    **Test sequence (perform on BOTH devices in order):**

    1. **<10s meal logging (CRITICAL — LOG-13):**
       - From a cold app state, tap into `/food-search`, search for a food, tap a result
       - Confirm the sheet shows the slot picker (pre-selected sensibly for the current time), quantity chips, live-scaling macro/CO2 display
       - Tap a quantity chip, tap "Log this food"
       - Confirm the sheet dismisses and an "Added to [Slot]" snackbar with Undo appears
       - Confirm the entry appears on `/dashboard` under the correct slot header
       - **Time the full sequence with a stopwatch across at least 3 attempts per device** — record each timing. All attempts must be under 10 seconds.

    2. **Merge behavior:**
       - Log the same food to the same slot a second time (same day) with the same unit
       - Confirm it merges into the existing entry (quantity increases) rather than creating a second row
       - Tap Undo on the resulting snackbar — confirm the entry returns to its pre-merge quantity (not deleted)

    3. **Recent & Favorites (LOG-07/LOG-08):**
       - Return to `/food-search` with an empty query — confirm the just-logged food appears under "Recent"
       - Tap the Recent row body (not the edit icon) — confirm it logs instantly with no sheet, with an Undo snackbar
       - Open a food's detail sheet, tap the favorite star — confirm it fills; confirm the food now appears under "Favorites" on the search empty state
       - Tap a Favorite row body — confirm instant one-tap log

    4. **Custom foods & overrides (LOG-10/LOG-11):**
       - From Settings → My Foods → "+ Add Custom Food", create a food with just a name and calorie value — confirm it saves and appears in the alphabetical list
       - Open an existing catalog food's detail sheet, tap "Edit this food" — confirm the override form pre-fills with the original's values; save it
       - Confirm searching for that food now shows only the override (not the original)
       - Re-open the override's edit form, tap "Revert to original" — confirm the original reappears in search

    5. **Managing logged entries (LOG-09):**
       - On `/dashboard`, swipe a logged entry — confirm Edit, Duplicate, Delete actions appear
       - Tap Duplicate — confirm a second identical entry appears in the same slot (not merged)
       - Tap Delete — confirm immediate removal with a "Deleted — Undo" snackbar; tap Undo — confirm the entry returns
       - Tap Edit — confirm the pre-filled sheet opens with the entry's current slot/quantity/unit

    6. **Airplane mode / offline (LOG-12):**
       - Enable airplane mode on the device
       - Repeat steps 1–5 in full — confirm every flow works identically with zero hangs, spinners, or silent failures

    **Expected results for PASS:**
    - Step 1: all timed attempts on both devices < 10 seconds — PASS
    - Steps 2–5: all behaviors match exactly as described — PASS
    - Step 6: full flow works identically offline — PASS
  </how-to-verify>
