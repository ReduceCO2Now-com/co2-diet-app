    **Test sequence (perform in order):**

    1. **Barcode scanner entry point:**
       - Open the app → go to the food search screen
       - Confirm a barcode/QR code scanner icon appears in the AppBar (top right)
       - Tap it → confirm the camera opens full-screen with a rectangular corner-bracket scanning guide centered on screen
       - Confirm the AppBar has a torch/flashlight toggle icon

    2. **Successful EAN-13 scan (CRITICAL — P0 criterion for LOG-03):**
       - Point camera at any packaged food product with an EAN-13 barcode
       - Confirm: camera freezes (live feed pauses), light haptic tap felt, loading spinner appears
       - Confirm: FoodDetailBottomSheet slides up showing product name, macro values
       - Confirm: CO₂e row shows a formatted value (e.g., "~2.3 kg CO₂e/kg") with a colored chip labeled "High" or "Medium"
       - Confirm: Tapping the chip opens an explanation bottom sheet with confidence band definition
       - Confirm: Explanation sheet contains "Full methodology" button
       - Tapping "Full methodology" → confirm MethodologyScreen opens with "AGRIBALYSE v3.1.1" and "Source ADEME" visible

    3. **Sheet dismiss → scanner resumes:**
       - Dismiss the FoodDetailBottomSheet (swipe down or tap outside)
       - Confirm: live camera feed resumes automatically (scanner is not stuck in frozen state)
       - Confirm: you can scan a second product without navigating back

    4. **No-match barcode flow (LOG-04):**
       - Find an unknown barcode (e.g., a home-printed QR label, a book ISBN, or an obscure product not in the seed DB)
       - Scan it
       - If the format is QR/ISBN/DataMatrix: confirm an inline snackbar message "That doesn't look like a product barcode" appears (no lookup triggered)
       - For an EAN barcode with no match: confirm the no-match screen appears with "No product found for this barcode" (or the network error variant if offline)
       - Confirm "Add as custom food" button is visible and tappable
       - Tap it → confirm navigation to the custom food stub route with the barcode pre-filled

    5. **Camera permission flow:**
       - On the Android device: go to Settings → Apps → CO₂ Diet → Permissions → Camera → Deny
       - Re-open the scanner from the app
       - Confirm: CameraPermissionDeniedWidget shows with "Camera access is needed to scan barcodes" and "Open Settings" button
       - Tap "Open Settings" → confirm Android settings app opens to the camera permission for the app
       - Grant permission again → confirm scanner resumes normally

    6. **iOS Simulator (optional UI check):**
       - Run on iOS Simulator: `flutter run -d <simulator_id>`
       - Navigate to barcode scanner — confirm the scanner screen renders (camera will show as black/unavailable — that's expected on Simulator)
       - Confirm permission-denied widget shows correctly in Simulator (Simulator has no real camera)
