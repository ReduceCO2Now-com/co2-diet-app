    On a real iOS or Android device (or an emulator/simulator with a real screen-reader engine — VoiceOver/TalkBack), with app storage cleared so onboarding is un-completed:
    1. Launch the app: Splash auto-advances to Welcome.
    2. Tap "Get Started" (or "Use Without Account") through Legal Consent; accept the 4 mandatory checkboxes; continue.

    5. Confirm Profile Setup appears next, still with no bottom nav bar visible.
    6. Fill in (or skip) profile fields; confirm the forward button reads "Go to Dashboard"; tap it.
    7. Confirm the Dashboard appears, the bottom nav bar is now visible, and there is no redirect loop back to Splash.
    8. Repeat steps 1-7 with VoiceOver (iOS) or TalkBack (Android) enabled; confirm each screen's elements are announced in a sensible reading order through the new Carousel → Profile Setup → Dashboard sequence, and that focus doesn't reach nav-bar elements before step 7.
    9. Force-quit and relaunch the app: confirm it opens directly to Dashboard (onboarding-complete state persisted), not back at Splash.
