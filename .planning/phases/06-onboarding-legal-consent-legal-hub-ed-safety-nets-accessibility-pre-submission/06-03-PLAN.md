<objective>
Build the shared ED (eating disorder) safety-net checker service and modal dialog, then wire it into the two existing trigger points CONTEXT.md locks: `ProfileScreen`'s calorie-target override dialog and `WeightScreen`'s target-weight field — plus add Profile Setup's unconditional Local Mode footer text.

Purpose: NFR-07/App Store Guideline 1.4.1 requires the app to refuse dangerously low calorie targets and BMI-implying goals without a warning + resource link. `TargetCalculator`'s existing 500-10000 kcal physiological clamp explicitly defers this UI-level warning to Phase 6 (see its doc comment). One shared component avoids threshold/copy drift between the two screens (06-CONTEXT.md's locked "two trigger points, one shared component" decision).

Output: `EdSafetyNetChecker` (pure Dart, unit-tested), `EdSafetyNetDialog` (shared modal widget), both existing screens' save flows gated by it, and `ProfileScreen`'s new unconditional footer text.
</objective>
